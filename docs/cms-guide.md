# Looking after your website

This guide is for whoever updates the Smart Move website. You do not need to
know anything technical. Nothing here can break the website, and if something
looks wrong you can always change it back.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **smartmove4u.co.uk/admin**
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Press **Login**

Bookmark that page. If you forget your password, use "Forgot password?" on the
sign-in screen.

Down the left-hand side you will see four sections:

- **Properties** — everything you are marketing
- **Website content** — the wording on your pages, your services, and your photos
- **Enquiries** — messages people have sent you
- **Settings** — your phone number, address, opening hours and menus

---

## Adding a property

1. Click **Properties**
2. Click **Create new** in the top right
3. Fill in the boxes on the **Property details** tab:

| Box                       | What to put                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Property title            | How you would describe it, e.g. "3 bedroom semi-detached house, Ashby" |
| Availability              | Leave as "Available to rent"                                           |
| Monthly rent              | Just the number, e.g. `725`. No pound sign.                            |
| Deposit                   | Optional. Leave blank if you would rather not show it.                 |
| Bedrooms                  | A number                                                               |
| Bathrooms                 | Optional                                                               |
| Property type             | Pick from the list                                                     |
| Area shown on the website | The area people search by, e.g. "Ashby, Scunthorpe"                    |
| Furnishing                | Optional                                                               |
| Available from            | Leave blank if it is available now                                     |
| One-line summary          | About 25 words. This shows on the property cards and in Google.        |
| Key features              | Short bullet points. Type one and press Enter to add it.               |
| Full description          | The main write-up                                                      |

4. Click the **Photos** tab and add your pictures (see below)
5. Click **Save**

That is it. The property is on the website immediately.

The web address and the date are filled in for you. You never need to touch
them.

### The other tabs

- **Address** — the full street address. This is for your records and for the
  map. Only the area and postcode are ever shown on the website.
- **More details** — EPC rating, council tax band, parking, garden, pets.
  Anything you leave blank simply is not shown.
- **SEO** — how the page appears in Google. You can ignore this; sensible
  wording is used automatically. Only fill it in if you want to override it.

## Adding photos to a property

1. Open the property and click the **Photos** tab
2. Click **Choose from existing** or drag your pictures onto the box
3. To upload new ones, click **Upload** and pick the files. You can select
   several at once.
4. For each new picture, type a short description in the **Describe this image**
   box, for example "Front of the house from the street". This is read aloud to
   people who use a screen reader, and it helps with Google.
5. Click **Save**

**The first photo is the main photo.** It is the one shown on the property
cards and when someone shares the link.

### Putting photos in a different order

On the Photos tab, drag a picture using the handle on its left-hand side and
drop it where you want it. Drag the one you want as the main photo to the top.
Then **Save**.

## Changing the rent

1. Click **Properties**
2. Click the property
3. Change the **Monthly rent** box
4. Click **Save**

The new figure is on the website straight away.

---

## Marking a property as let agreed

1. Open the property
2. Change **Availability** to **Let agreed**
3. Click **Save**

It stays on the website with a "Let agreed" label, which shows people you are
active and busy.

## Taking a property off the website

1. Open the property
2. Change **Availability** to **Let — hide from the website**
3. Click **Save**

The property disappears from the website but stays in your list, so you can put
it back up later without typing it all again.

Use **Not ready yet — hide from the website** for a property you are still
writing up.

## Deleting a property for good

Open the property, click the **⋯** button next to Save, and choose **Delete**.
This cannot be undone, so hiding is usually the better choice.

## Changing the words on a page

1. Click **Website content**, then **Website pages**
2. Click the page you want, for example **Landlords**
3. Edit the text
4. Click **Save**

Each page is built from **sections**. You can:

- **Change a section** — click it to open it and edit the text
- **Reorder sections** — drag them using the handle on the left
- **Add a section** — click **Add Section** at the bottom and pick a type
- **Remove a section** — click the **⋯** on the section and choose Remove

The section types are:

| Type                  | What it does                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Text section          | A heading and some writing                             |
| List of features      | A grid of short points with titles                     |
| Numbered steps        | A "how it works" sequence                              |
| Photo with text       | A picture next to some writing                         |
| Call to action        | A coloured band with a heading and buttons             |
| Available properties  | Shows some of your current properties                  |
| Questions and answers | A list people can click to expand                      |
| Our contact details   | Your address, phone and hours, pulled in automatically |
| Enquiry form          | A form people can fill in                              |

### The home page

The home page has its own screen because it is laid out differently. Click
**Website content**, then **Home Page**. The tabs across the top match the
sections as you scroll down the page.

### Choosing which properties appear on the home page

Open a property, and on the right-hand side tick **Show on the home page**.
If you tick fewer than the home page shows, the newest properties fill the rest.

## Changing your contact details

1. Click **Settings**, then **Business Details**
2. Change your phone number, email, address or opening hours
3. Click **Save**

These appear at the top and bottom of every page, on the contact page, and in
Google. Changing them here changes them everywhere at once.

The **Company and compliance** tab is where your redress scheme and Client
Money Protection membership go. Letting agents have to display these by law, so
please fill them in before the site goes live.

---

## Changing the menu

1. Click **Settings**, then **Website Settings**
2. Click the **Main menu** tab
3. Drag items to reorder, or click **Add Menu item**
4. Click **Save**

For **Where it goes**, type the page address starting with a slash, for example
`/landlords` or `/properties`.

---

## Reading your enquiries

1. Click **Enquiries**

You will see everything people have sent, newest first. Click one to read it.

Each enquiry shows the person's name, email, phone number and message. Property
enquiries also show which property they were looking at.

When you have replied, tick **Dealt with** on the right and click **Save**, so
you know what is still outstanding. There is also a **Your notes** box for
anything you want to remember. The sender never sees your notes.

You will also get an email each time someone sends an enquiry, so you do not
have to keep checking here.

### Deleting an enquiry

If someone asks you to remove their details, open the enquiry, click the **⋯**
next to Save, and choose **Delete**. That removes it completely.

## Adding a service

1. Click **Website content**, then **Services**
2. Click **Create new**
3. Fill in the service name, a one-line summary, and pick an icon
4. Add sections the same way as a page
5. Click **Save**

The number in **Position in the list** controls the order. Lower numbers come
first.

---

## Adding someone to the website admin

1. Click **Settings**, then **Users**
2. Click **Create new**
3. Enter their name, email address and a password
4. Choose what they can do:
   - **Manage properties, pages and enquiries** — for most staff
   - **Manage everything, including staff accounts** — only for people you
     fully trust
5. Click **Save**

The first account created when the site was set up cannot be deleted. That is
deliberate, so you cannot lock yourself out.

On a private preview, the Dashboard also has a **Load demo properties** button.
It copies example listings from another agency so the site can be demonstrated.
Do not use it on the live Smart Move website, and delete those listings before
launch.

---

## If something goes wrong

**I saved something by mistake.** Pages and services keep a history. Open the
page and look for **Versions** to go back to an earlier one. Properties do not
keep a history, so re-type the correct value and save.

**A property is not showing on the website.** Check its **Availability**. Only
"Available to rent" and "Let agreed" appear publicly.

**My photo will not upload.** Files must be under 15 MB and be a JPG, PNG or
WebP. Phone photos are usually fine. If one is too big, email it to yourself
and choose a smaller size.

**The website looks broken.** Ring your developer. Nothing you can do in here
can break the website's layout, so it is almost certainly something else.
